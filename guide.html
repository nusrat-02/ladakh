Travel Guide

A few simple tips before visiting Ladakh.

Prepare for Your Journey

Many visitors prefer the warmer months, generally from May to September. Always check local weather and road conditions before travelling.

Carry warm clothes, comfortable shoes, water, sun protection and required travel documents.

Best Time

Warmer months are generally more comfortable for many visitors.

What to Carry

Warm clothes, comfortable shoes, water and sun protection.

Travel Slowly

Allow time to acclimatise to the high altitude and follow local advice.
